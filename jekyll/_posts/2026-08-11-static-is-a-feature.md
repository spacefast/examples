  <h1>Static is a feature</h1>
  <p class="lede">The fastest request is often the one your application never has to handle.</p>
  <p>Prebuilt pages are easy to cache, easy to inspect, and pleasantly difficult to wake up at three in the morning. Use a server where the request changes the answer. Let files handle the rest.</p>
