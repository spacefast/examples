Maintenance is a product surface

The future maintainer is already one of your users.

Product work does not end when the first person succeeds. Someone still has to understand the logs, rotate a credential, replace a dependency, and decide whether an alert matters at two in the morning.

Those moments have interfaces: names, defaults, error messages, and escape hatches. Treating them as leftovers makes a product expensive in exactly the years when it should be becoming dependable.

The maintenance experience is the product seen across time.

Leave the next person a system that explains its current state and the safest next action. That next person may be you.
