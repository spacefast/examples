Carry less. Notice more.

The small kit, trail etiquette, and turnaround rules our crew uses on every North Ridge sweep.

01

Pack for the pause

Bring one warm layer even in August. A mechanical issue becomes a cold hour quickly when the ridge wind arrives.

  • One litre of water per person
  • Paper map and charged phone
  • Shell, warm layer, and headlamp
02

Protect the tread

Stay on the established line. If you meet standing water, pass through its centre instead of widening the trail around it.

  • Yield to uphill traffic
  • Keep wheels off soft shoulders
  • Pack out food and repair waste
03

Turn around early

Weather moves fast above Granite Marker 12. Low cloud, thunder, or a cooling wind are reasons to descend, not problems to solve.

  • Leave the summit by 3 p.m.
  • Never cross a closed gate
  • Tell someone your return time

If something goes wrong

Call local emergency services first. Trail markers show a route name and number; include both, then send a second report to the volunteer crew at crew@northridge.example.
