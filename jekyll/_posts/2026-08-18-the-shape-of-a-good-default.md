  <h1>The shape of a good default</h1>
  <p class="lede">A good default is opinionated enough to help and quiet enough to disappear.</p>
  <p>Defaults are product decisions wearing plain clothes. They decide who gets momentum, which mistakes become common, and how much context a person must carry before the useful work begins.</p>
  <blockquote>Make the right path the short path, then leave an honest door open for the exceptional case.</blockquote>
  <p>The strongest defaults are reversible. They offer a clear result now without making tomorrow's decision expensive.</p>
